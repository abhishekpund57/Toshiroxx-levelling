45-minute walk

Treadmill plan

Incline walking burns more fat than flat walking and is easy on the knees.

Do it after weights

Lift first while you have energy, then walk. Your body then burns more stored fat during the walk.

Weekly progression

WeeksSpeedIncline

3–45.0–5.5 km/h5–7%

Move up only when the current level feels comfortable. You should breathe harder but still be able to talk in short sentences.

Structure

  • First 5 min: flat, easy pace
  • Middle: your incline + speed from the table
  • Last 5 min: incline back to 0, slow down

Form tips

  • Don't hold the handrails. It cuts the calories you burn a lot. Lower the incline instead.
  • Stand tall, chest up, arms swinging naturally.
  • Normal steps — don't over-stride.

How to lose belly, chest & face fat

The honest truth

No exercise burns fat from one body part. Crunches won't target belly fat and nothing targets face fat. Fat comes off everywhere at once when you eat fewer calories than you burn. The gym builds muscle so you look firm and lean underneath.

Food matters most

  • Protein in every meal — eggs, paneer, chicken, fish, dal, curd, soya chunks, whey.
  • Cut liquid sugar — cold drinks, packaged juice, sweet chai/coffee.
  • Reduce fried snacks & sweets — samosa, namkeen, biscuits, mithai.
  • Half your plate vegetables, smaller portion of rice/roti.
  • Drink 3–4 litres of water a day.

Face fat & puffiness

  • Sleep 7–8 hours — poor sleep causes puffiness and cravings.
  • Less salt and processed food reduces water retention.
  • Limit alcohol. The face often slims a bit after the belly — be patient.

Chest fat

Overall fat loss + chest training firms the area. If you feel a firm, rubbery lump behind the nipple that doesn't shrink with weight loss, it may be gynecomastia — show it to a doctor.

Getting stronger

  • Pick a weight where the last 2 reps feel hard but form stays clean.
  • When all sets feel easy, go up to the next weight.
  • Note your weights in your phone so you remember next week.

Safety

  • Sharp pain = stop. Muscle burn/soreness = normal.
  • Never hold your breath — breathe out while pushing/pulling.
  • Ask gym staff to help adjust a machine seat.

Expect visible change in 6–8 weeks if you stay consistent with gym and food.
